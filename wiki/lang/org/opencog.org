* Executive Summary
The OpenCog project represents a comprehensive effort to develop Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) through an integrated cognitive architecture. This document outlines the organizational requirements, technical specifications, and development roadmap for achieving human-level and beyond artificial intelligence.

** Mission Statement
To create an open-source framework for AGI that combines symbolic reasoning, neural networks, evolutionary algorithms, and cognitive architectures into a unified system capable of general intelligence.

** Core Objectives
- Develop scalable cognitive architecture
- Implement neural-symbolic integration
- Create embodied AI capabilities
- Foster collaborative research community
- Maintain modular, extensible design
