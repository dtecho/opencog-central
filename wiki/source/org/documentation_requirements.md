
# Documentation Requirements for Complete Organizational Persona Model

## Priority 1: Governance & Decision-Making
**Needed Documents**:
- Project governance structure
- Technical steering committee processes
- Community decision-making examples
- Conflict resolution procedures
- Release planning and approval processes

**Rationale**: Essential for understanding how the organization operates and makes decisions

## Priority 2: Community Dynamics
**Needed Documents**:
- Community interaction guidelines
- Contributor onboarding materials
- Meeting notes or community calls
- Mailing list archives (representative samples)
- Discord/forum interaction examples

**Rationale**: Critical for understanding communication patterns and community culture

## Priority 3: Historical Context
**Needed Documents**:
- Major milestone achievements
- Significant challenges and responses
- Evolution of the project vision
- Key technical breakthroughs
- Community growth patterns

**Rationale**: Provides context for organizational resilience and adaptability

## Priority 4: External Relationships
**Needed Documents**:
- Academic collaboration examples
- Industry partnership approaches
- Conference presentations or papers
- User feedback and testimonials
- Integration success stories

**Rationale**: Illuminates how the organization engages with external stakeholders

## Priority 5: Cultural Artifacts
**Needed Documents**:
- Informal communication examples
- Humor and personality indicators
- Celebration or recognition practices
- Shared terminology or "inside language"
- Community traditions or rituals

**Rationale**: Captures the authentic personality and cultural nuances

## Methodology for Collection
1. **Systematic Review**: Request specific document types in order of priority
2. **Pattern Analysis**: Look for recurring themes and language patterns
3. **Behavioral Examples**: Collect concrete instances of organizational responses
4. **Community Input**: Gather feedback from long-term contributors
5. **Iterative Refinement**: Update framework based on new insights

## Success Metrics
- Consistency in AI responses across different contexts
- Recognition by community members as "authentic" OpenCog voice
- Effective communication with diverse stakeholder groups
- Maintenance of technical accuracy while being approachable
